# SA Coffee Compliance Guide

> Regulatory context for importing and distributing coffee in South Africa, and exporting from Rwanda.

## South Africa Requirements

### Business Registration
- **CIPC**: Register a company (Pty Ltd recommended for liability protection)
- **SARS**: Tax clearance, VAT registration (mandatory above R1M turnover)
- **DTI**: BEE certificate may be required for certain contracts

### Food Safety
- **DoH**: R638 Regulations Governing General Hygiene Requirements for Food Premises
- **FSCA**: If selling to FSP practitioners, additional FAIS requirements may apply
- **Labelling**: R146 Foodstuffs, Cosmetics and Disinfectants Act — ingredient, origin, allergen labelling

### Import/Export
- **SARS Customs**: Tariff heading 0901 (coffee, unroasted) or 0902 (roasted)
- **Phytosanitary certificate**: Required for green coffee import
- **Certificate of Origin**: Required for preferential tariff (if applicable under SACU/EAC agreements)
- **Permit**: May require import permit from DAFF (Department of Agriculture)

## Rwanda Export Requirements

- **NAEB**: Rwanda National Agricultural Export Development Board — exporter registration
- **Coffee export licence**: Required for all coffee exporters
- **Quality certification**: Rwanda Coffee Authority cupping and grading
- **Traceability**: Required for specialty coffee — washing station, cooperative, farm lot

## Compliance Gate Checklist

- [ ] SA business registered (CIPC)
- [ ] SARS tax clearance obtained
- [ ] Food premises registered (municipality)
- [ ] Import permit obtained (if required)
- [ ] Rwanda exporter verified (NAEB directory)
- [ ] Phytosanitary certificate route confirmed
- [ ] Labelling compliance confirmed
- [ ] Insurance (cargo, product liability) confirmed
