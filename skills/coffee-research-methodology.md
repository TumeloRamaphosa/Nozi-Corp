## Coffee-Specific Rules

- Cross-reference with existing roastery shortlist (`RESEARCH/JOHANNESBURG_COFFEE_ROASTERIES.md`).
- Verify Rwanda supplier status via NAEB directory.
- Include demo disclaimer on all briefs.
- Public sources only for SA regulatory context.
