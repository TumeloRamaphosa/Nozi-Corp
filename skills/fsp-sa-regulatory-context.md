## Demo Checklist Themes

### Intake & Record-Keeping
- Client goals, financial situation, and risk tolerance captured (even as demo placeholders)
- Identification and conflict-of-interest placeholders documented
- Advice scope defined before needs analysis

### Conduct (TCF-Aligned Themes)
- Clear, fair, not misleading communications
- Suitability and needs analysis before product discussion (in real practice)
- Fees and conflicts disclosed where relevant

### Marketing / Reports
- Demo disclaimer on every artefact
- No performance guarantees
- Distinguish facts, assumptions, and opinions

## Agent Usage

- Cite public sources when mentioning regulation
